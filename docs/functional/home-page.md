# Home Page

**Route:** `/`

The home page is the entry point for hosts. It introduces the product and explains the creation flow before directing the host to the dashboard.

---

## Content

The page displays:

- A headline and short description of what Mystery Date does.
- A three-step explainer of how the product works:
  1. **Plan the surprise** — decide what the final reveal will be.
  2. **Add three riddles** — hide the surprise behind clues only your partner will understand.
  3. **Share the unlock link** — your partner unlocks the experience one riddle at a time.
- A primary call-to-action button: **Create a mystery date**, which navigates to `/create`.
- A note that no account is needed.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Clicking **Create a mystery date** navigates the user to the Host Dashboard (`/create`).
- The page has no interactive state. It is purely informational.
